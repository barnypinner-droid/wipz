Help us test Wipz

You're one of the first people to try Wipz before anyone else. It takes about 10 minutes: install the app, try a real wipz pot, and tell us what worked, and what didn't.

Step 1

Get the app

Android only for now. iPhone testing is coming once we're further along.

Download for Android
Heads up before you tap that

The next page has its own button that says Install. Tapping it does not install the app, it only downloads the file. You still need to do step 2 below afterward.

  1. Tap Download for Android above, then tap Install on the page that opens. This only downloads the file, nothing installs yet.
  2. Go into your Downloads folder and install it from there. This is the step that actually installs it.
  3. If asked whether to trust this app, say yes, it's a trusted app.
  4. Open Wipz.
Step 2

Create your account

First time opening the app, here's exactly what happens.

  1. Tap Sign up, enter your email and choose a password, then tap the sign-up button to submit.
  2. Check your email for a confirmation link, it usually takes a minute or two to arrive.
  3. Tap the link in that email to confirm your account.
  4. Go back to the app, tap Sign in, and enter the same email and password.
The confirmation email comes from Supabase, not Wipz

The sender will show as something like noreply@mail.app.supabase.io, not Wipz. That's expected for now, it's not spam, please don't ignore or delete it. If it doesn't arrive within a couple of minutes, check your junk or spam folder.

Optional: faster sign-in with Face ID or fingerprint

If your phone already has Face ID or fingerprint unlock set up, Wipz will automatically offer to use it to unlock the app each time you open it after this. Nothing to set up separately, just say yes when asked.

Step 3

What to actually try

Don't just look around: run through the real flow so your feedback means something.

  • Create a wipz pot: pick recurring, ad-hoc, or savings goal
  • Add someone from your contacts
  • Pay into the pot
  • Check the activity log: can you see who's paid and how much?
  • If you're the organiser, try logging a withdrawal
Test mode: no real money moves

This build uses Stripe's test card, so nothing is actually charged. When asked for card details, use 4242 4242 4242 4242, any future expiry date, and any 3-digit CVC.
