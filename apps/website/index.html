The problem

The organiser penalty is real

💸

One person fronts it

Pitch hire, a table booking, a villa deposit — someone pays upfront and spends weeks chasing repayment over text.

🕵️

No shared visibility

When one person holds the kitty, nobody else can see what it's being spent on — that's how trust breaks down.

📉

Flat subs don't work

Not everyone plays every week, so a flat standing order over-charges some and under-collects for the pitch.

The solution

Three ways to wipz

Every wipz pot carries a stated purpose, agreed rules, and a full activity log — nothing leaves the pot quietly.

Recurring

Weekly five-a-side subs

RSVP-gated: the payment request only goes out on weeks you've confirmed you're playing. No charge if you're not in.

Ad-hoc

"Shall we wipz £100?"

Anyone proposes an amount — the group pays in and watches the pot fill live before the night out.

Savings goal

Group holiday fund

A target and deadline. Funds are locked until the group agrees to book.

Getting money in and out

However you like to pay

Every wipz pot takes the payment methods you already use — and lets the group spend straight from the pot when it's time.

Pay in, your way

Apple Pay, Google Pay, debit or credit card, PayPal, bank transfer, or Pay by Bank. Whatever's already on your phone works — no new account numbers to remember.
